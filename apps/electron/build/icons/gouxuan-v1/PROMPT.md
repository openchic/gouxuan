# 内置 ImageGen 视觉参考提示词

模式为内置 ImageGen 编辑，参考图是用户已选定的尖端回钩概念稿。正式 SVG 保留登录页已试用的同一轮廓；不将 AI 位图嵌入 SVG 冒充矢量源文件。

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Asset type: macOS application icon, unmasked square master artwork
Input images: Image 1 is the edit target: the user-selected sharp returning-hook logo.
Primary request: turn this logo into clean production-ready application icon artwork. Change the jade shape to uniform #1b1b1b and the ivory backdrop to pure #ffffff. Preserve the exact recognizable hook silhouette: the thin pointed upper-left start, thick curved right spine, open left side, rounded bottom return, and pointed inward tip. Do not invent a G, letter, circle, or another symbol.
Composition/framing: one centered hook on a square, full-bleed opaque white canvas. The hook occupies about 64% of canvas width and 67% of canvas height, with balanced safe margins. Flat front view, no perspective. Produce a single square image, not a presentation board.
Style/medium: flat, crisp vector-like monochrome logo. Solid near-black foreground, pure white background, no texture.
Constraints: keep the established shape and proportions; no text, no border, no watermark, no gradient, no bevel, no shadow, no glossy lighting, no pre-rounded canvas corners, no extra elements. This is unmasked master artwork; macOS applies its own final mask in the modern layered pipeline.
```
